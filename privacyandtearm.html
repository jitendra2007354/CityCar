Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 9, 2026

Kids Quiz (the "App"), developed by Jitendra Singh Chauhan, is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

1. Information Collection and Use

The App works primarily offline. We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) such as names or email addresses unless you voluntarily provide them. However, we use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

A. Google AdMob (Advertising)

We use AdMob to serve ads. For users identified as children (under 13), we implement code to disable the Advertising ID (AAID) and serve non-personalized ads in compliance with COPPA and Google Play Families Policies.

B. Firebase Analytics & Cloud Messaging (FCM)

We use Firebase to understand app usage and send notifications. Data collected automatically includes device metadata (OS version, model), app install source, and basic interaction events. This data is used to improve the service and prevent abuse.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Since this App is designed for kids and mixed audiences, we strictly adhere to the following:

  • We do not collect precise location data from children.
  • We use a "Neutral Age Screen" to ensure younger users receive child-safe ads.
  • We do not transmit sensitive identifiers (like AAID) from users identified as children.

3. Data Storage

User progress, badges, and age settings are stored locally on your device. We do not upload this data to our servers.
