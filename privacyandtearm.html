Privacy Policy

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how City Car Ajmer ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information through the Kids Quiz mobile application. We prioritize the privacy of families and children.

2. Child Safety & COPPA Compliance

The Kids Quiz app is explicitly designed and intended for children under the age of 13. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not collect, track, or store any personal data, and we completely disable the tracking of Advertising IDs (AAID/GAID) to ensure an absolute safe experience.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide a high-quality, safe experience, we utilize the following Google services:

  • FCM

    Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): Used strictly to send functional push notifications using randomized tokens that do not identify individual users or collect personal data.

  • ANL

    Google Analytics: Collects completely anonymous, aggregated metadata (such as device model and app crash logs) to help us fix bugs and improve application performance. No personal information is tracked.

  • ADS

    Google AdMob: Serves strictly contextual, non-personalized, family-safe ads. All ad requests are marked as child-directed (TFCD / TAG_FOR_CHILD_DIRECTED_TREATMENT) to ensure absolute compliance with Google Play's Families Policy requirements.

4. Local Data Storage

The App is designed for offline functionality. Your scores, math progress, and badges are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.
