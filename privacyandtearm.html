Privacy Policy

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how City Car Ajmer ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information through the Kids Quiz mobile application. We prioritize the privacy of families and children.

2. Child Safety & COPPA Compliance

For users under the age of 13, we strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not collect personalized data, we disable Advertising IDs (AAID), and we use a Neutral Age Screen to ensure a safe experience.

3. Third-Party Services

To provide a high-quality experience, we utilize the following Google Cloud services:

  • FCM

    Firebase Cloud Messaging: Used to send push notifications. We use randomized tokens that do not identify you personally.

  • ANL

    Google Analytics: Collects anonymous metadata (device model, app crashes) to help us fix bugs and improve performance.

  • ADS

    Google AdMob: Serves contextual, non-personalized ads to children and personalized ads to adult users (18+) who have provided consent.

4. Local Data Storage

The App is designed for offline functionality. Your scores, math progress, and badges are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.
