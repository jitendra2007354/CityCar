Our Story: Trust is Our Fuel.

City Car Ajmer began as a local family-owned service in 2013. We noticed a significant gap in the Ajmer transport market: international and domestic pilgrims were struggling with unreliable drivers and inconsistent pricing.

We dedicated ourselves to transparency. No hidden charges, no commissions from shops, and absolute punctuality. Today, we are proud to be Ajmer's #1 rated taxi service by travelers worldwide.

About City Car Ajmer

City Car Ajmer is a premier transportation provider based in the heart of Rajasthan. Unlike car dealerships, we specialize exclusively in Taxi Services and Car Rentals for tourists and locals.
