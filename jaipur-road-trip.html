Ajmer to Jaipur Road Trip: Highway Stops & Sights

Published by City Car Ajmer

The drive between Ajmer and the Pink City of Jaipur is one of the most frequently traveled routes in Rajasthan. Spanning roughly 135 kilometers along the NH48, it is a smooth, well-maintained six-lane expressway that makes for a comfortable and enjoyable road trip. Whether you are traveling for business, catching a flight at Jaipur International Airport, or embarking on a heritage tour, this guide will help you make the most of your journey.

The Kishangarh Marble Market

Shortly after leaving Ajmer, you will pass through Kishangarh, famous across Asia for its massive marble market. While it is primarily an industrial hub, the sheer scale of the marble yards is a sight to behold. For photography enthusiasts, the "Kishangarh Dumping Yard" has recently become a viral location. The accumulated marble slurry has created a surreal, snow-white landscape that looks exactly like a winter wonderland in the middle of Rajasthan.

Best Highway Dhabas and Rest Stops

A road trip in North India is incomplete without a stop at a traditional highway Dhaba. The NH48 route offers several excellent culinary rest stops:

  • Hotel Highway King (Dudu): Located perfectly at the halfway point, this is the most popular rest stop for families. It offers clean restrooms, ample parking, and delicious vegetarian Rajasthani and Punjabi food.
  • Sharma Dhaba: Known for its authentic, spicy local food and Kulhad Chai. It's a great place to stretch your legs and enjoy a quick snack.

Navigating the Toll Plazas

The NH48 route is a toll road. If you are driving your own vehicle, ensure your FASTag is properly funded to avoid long queues in the cash lanes. If you are booking a cab, standard practice dictates that toll taxes are either included in your initial quote or paid directly at the booth. At City Car Ajmer, our one-way and round-trip quotes always include transparent toll breakdowns.

Approaching Jaipur

As you near Jaipur, you will have multiple entry options depending on your final destination. If you are heading towards Vaishali Nagar or the Airport, the bypass roads offer a traffic-free route. If you are traveling towards the heritage center (Hawa Mahal, City Palace), you will enter through the main arterial roads which can get quite congested during peak evening hours.
